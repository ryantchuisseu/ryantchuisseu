## Posting conventions
- Title = the calendar title (English). YouTube Shorts title: `<Title> 😳 | Part 1`.
- First comment (pinned): "Which story should get a Part 2? 👇"
- Hashtags (3-5): #storytime #mystery #blocktales #shorts #animatedstory
- End each video on the unresolved last line (no "like and subscribe" over the twist).
- Part 2 slots (days 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56): pick the story with the best watch-time + shares in the last 7 days.
- Turn on the platform's "AI-generated content" label if the visuals or voice are synthetic.

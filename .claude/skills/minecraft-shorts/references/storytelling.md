# Story formula (extracted from two viral blocky/Roblox shorts, ~2 min each)

Observed on two videos with ~1M likes: a one-rule world, one hero with a personal stake, a visible countdown, a twist. Two samples only: treat as a hypothesis to test, not a law.

```
0-10 s    HOOK       Visible anomaly + the rule or mystery (a counter above every head; the only human left untouched)
10-40 s   WORLD      2-3 quick examples of the rule (gag or strangeness) — society shown, never lectured
40-70 s   HERO       One named character, a personal stake (he is about to lose something / someone)
70-100 s  COUNTDOWN  A visible number going down (300→0, 10→2): measurable tension
100-125 s REVEAL     Loophole in the rule OR hidden truth, then a collective payoff or cliffhanger
```

## Principles
- **One rule per world**, shown by the image in < 10 s; the regime/idea is the *setting*, discovered through the story, not the subject explained by a narrator.
- **One hero followed throughout**, first-person or close third; short sentences.
- **Visible number or counter** in the set (sign board, floating tag, chat line).
- **Comedy first, danger second.**
- **End on a reveal or cliffhanger** that makes the viewer follow for part 2.
- Hook mini-variants: anomaly ("everyone but him…"), counter ("1,000,000 left"), tour ("4 bunkers, 4 rules").

## Caption style
1–3 words per chunk (single yellow word also works), huge, centered, thick black outline, keyword in uppercase or yellow. Set `E.CAP.y` (0.2 top / 0.5 center / 0.78 low), `E.CAP.hl` (highlight colour), `E.HL=[words]` in voice mode.

## Audio
Music bed by mood (`happy|calm|tense|epic`), whoosh on cuts, thud on stamps. Voice-over (user's MP3) ducks the music automatically.

## Anti-patterns
Explaining the concept over a static scene; more than ~2.5 s without a visual change; captions over the face; fake income/results claims in any hook.

## Script anatomy (measured on the full English script of video 3, ~1M-view style, 119 s)
378 words → **3.2 words/s**, 40 sentences, **9.4 words/sentence**. Target for a 2-min episode: 360-400 words (a 60 s episode: ~190).

| Time | Beat | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 0-16 s | **Rule + absurd fact** ("you're never allowed to touch the water. And the craziest part…") | Prohibition in sentence 1, an open loop in sentence 2 |
| 16-31 s | **Narrator + daily life** (name, "12 platforms every morning", zip lines, bridges, playground) | Concrete number, plants every prop that pays off later |
| 31-36 s | **Rule restated + name of the threat** ("We call them the Watchers") | Gives the danger a name |
| 36-57 s | **Two-level escalation** ("But that's nothing compared to the dark zone") + "Nobody goes there anymore. At least, nobody is supposed to." | Plants the forbidden place |
| 57-71 s | **Incident with the friend** ("suddenly, crack!") | First loss of control |
| 71-92 s | **Rescue + collapse** (threat approaches, platform breaks) | Tension at its peak |
| 92-107 s | **Forced choice** ("We had no choice") → the planted zip line leads into the forbidden zone, then it breaks | The set-up pays off; no way back |
| 107-119 s | **Whisper + twist** ("Look at the water… someone who looked exactly like me") | Last 5 words = the hook for part 2 |

Techniques: every prop is mentioned early and used later (zip lines at ~20 s → escape at ~95 s; dark zone at ~40 s → entered at ~96 s); "But" pivots every ~20 s; the hero is named at 16 s and the friend at 57 s; ends unresolved.
Template for the opening: *"In this [place], you're never allowed to [rule]. And the craziest part, [absurd fact about the world]."*

## Second measured script (zombie/bunker story, 128 s)
~370 words, ~2.9 words/s. Same engine as the water-town story:
| Beat | Zombie/bunker story | Water-town story |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (sentence 1) | "everyone has a bunker, **except me**" (exclusion) | "you're **never allowed** to touch the water" (prohibition) |
| Escalating tour | 4 numbered bunkers, each stranger than the last (glass/ocean → luxury → silence → 100 doors, last never opened) | 2 threats, "that's nothing compared to the dark zone" |
| Hero's anomaly | the zombies never attack me | the old zip line leads into the forbidden zone |
| Mystery object | the unopenable last door → **Bunker 100** opens when touched | the dark zone |
| Reveal | "That baby was me… built to protect everyone **from me**" (the hero is the threat) | someone under the water who looks exactly like me |
| Last line | countdown 10-9-8 + "Phase 2 has begun" | whisper + doppelgänger |
Common engine: **bounded strange world → hero who doesn't fit → forbidden/odd place → reveal that reframes everything (hero = the secret) → countdown / unresolved last line.** Rule of thumb: the twist re-explains an early detail (bunkers' purpose, "never touch the water").
